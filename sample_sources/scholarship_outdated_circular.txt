SUBJECT: NATIONAL MEANS-CUM-MERIT SCHOLARSHIP SCHEME FOR ACADEMIC YEAR 2023-24

1. The Ministry invites applications for the academic year 2023-24 under the National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship Scheme.
2. The income ceiling of parents should not exceed Rs. 2,50,000/- per annum for the 2023-24 cycle.
3. The scholarship amount is Rs. 12,000 per annum paid via PFMS portal.
4. The deadline for online application submission for cycle 2023-24 is 30th September 2023.
5. All applicants must attach domicile certificates and signed declaration forms.
