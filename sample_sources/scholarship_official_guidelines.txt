GOVERNMENT OF INDIA
MINISTRY OF EDUCATION
DEPARTMENT OF SCHOOL EDUCATION & LITERACY
SCHOLARSHIPS DIVISION

NATIONAL MERIT-CUM-MEANS SCHOLARSHIP SCHEME (NMMSS)
OFFICIAL IMPLEMENTATION GUIDELINES FOR ACADEMIC YEAR 2025-26
Website: https://scholarships.gov.in
Date of Notification: 15-07-2025

1. OBJECTIVE:
The objective of the scheme is to award scholarships to meritorious students of economically weaker sections to arrest their drop out at class VIII and encourage them to continue the study at secondary stage.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Students whose parental income from all sources is not more than Rs. 3,50,000/- per annum are eligible to avail the scholarships. The student must have minimum 55% marks or equivalent grade in Class VII examination (relaxable by 5% for SC/ST students). The students studying in Kendriya Vidyalayas and Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas are not entitled to get scholarship under this scheme.

3. SCHOLARSHIP BENEFIT AMOUNT:
The amount of scholarship is Rs. 12,000/- per annum (Rs. 1,000/- per month) selected students from class IX to XII in State Government, Government-aided and local body schools. The scholarship is disbursed directly into bank accounts through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

4. APPLICATION DEADLINE:
The closing date for submission of online applications on the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) for the academic cycle 2025-26 is 31st October 2025. Verification by Institute Nodal Officer must be completed by 15th November 2025.

5. APPLICATION FEE:
There is no application fee for applying to this scholarship scheme through the National Scholarship Portal.

6. MANDATORY DOCUMENTS:
Mandatory documents include Aadhaar Card, Previous Year Marksheet, Bank Passbook copy, and Valid Income Certificate issued by Competent Revenue Authority.

7. SELECTION PROCESS:
Selection will be made on the basis of the State Level Mental Ability Test (MAT) and Scholastic Aptitude Test (SAT) conducted by each State/UT.

8. RENEWAL POLICY:
For renewal of scholarship in Class X, XI and XII, the awardee must pass the previous class with at least 55% marks (50% for SC/ST) in first attempt.
