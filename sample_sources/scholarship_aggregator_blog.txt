National Means Scholarship 2025 Application Form, Eligibility & Dates

Great news for school students! National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship applications are now live. Students looking for financial help can apply online today. Here is the complete summary of criteria:

- Eligibility: All school students who passed 7th grade can apply.
- Income Ceiling: Parents annual income must not exceed Rs. 1,50,000 per year from all sources. (Note: Many students got rejected last year due to income proof issues).
- Scholarship Amount: Selected candidates will receive Rs. 10,000 yearly stipend deposited into savings accounts.
- Application Deadline: Last date for application submission is 15th November 2025 according to our sources.
- Application Fee: Registration is completely free for all categories.
- Documents: Passport photo, caste certificate, and school ID card required.

Disclaimer: This is an unofficial informational guide. Candidates are advised to check local district education offices for clarifications.
